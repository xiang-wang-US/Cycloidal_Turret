# 18:1 cycloidal drive (azimuth)

`cd18t1` is the 18:1 single stage cycloidal reducer that turns the whole head on the azimuth axis, driven by a NEMA 17 stepper.
The base motor rotates with the head so the turret can sweep 360 degrees without twisting its wiring.

## Files

- `18-1 Cycloidal Drive Assembly.STEP`: full reducer assembly with motor envelope

## Build notes

- Same Desmos to SolidWorks profile workflow as the 12:1 drive, with a higher pin count for the larger ratio
- Added after the first direct drive prototype could not rotate the head
